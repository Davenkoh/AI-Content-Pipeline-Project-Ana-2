🇯🇵 OVERRATED vs UNDERRATED places in Japan

Japan has a few spots that get hyped constantly online, but after going to a lot of them myself, some are genuinely worth it and some are not. Here are my honest overrated versus underrated takes, plus a real fact behind each one.

Shibuya Sky gets recommended everywhere, but the ticket is about $20 and the deck is packed shoulder to shoulder at sunset. The Tokyo Government Building observatory has the same skyline, Shibuya included, and it's completely free.

Shibuya Crossing is iconic and worth crossing once, but it's smaller and over quicker than the videos make it look. The crowd is honestly the whole show. Shimokitazawa is the area nearby that nobody expects to fall for, with vintage shops, tiny cafes, and record stores instead of a photo op.

Takeshita Street is one loud, crowded lane of tourist crepes. It's fun for about half an hour, but Cat Street right next door is where the actual Tokyo fashion is, without the crowds.

Tokyo Skytree is pricey, packed, and a bit far out for what it is. Mori Tower in Roppongi Hills is an easier ticket, cheaper, less packed, and gives a closer view of Tokyo Tower.

The two hour queue for Ichiran is mostly for the algorithm, not the ramen itself. Omoide Yokocho in Shinjuku has tiny yakitori bars where skewers run about 150 to 300 yen each, with no wait at all.

These are just my honest opinions, so let me know if you disagree. Save this before your Japan trip.
