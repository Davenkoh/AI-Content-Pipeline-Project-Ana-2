## Unverified / caveats for the human

1. **Universal Studios entry price (slide 6, "8,900 yen to get in").** USJ prices dynamically by
   date band, currently roughly 8,900 to 10,900 yen for an adult 1-Day Studio Pass. 8,900 is the
   low end, so the slide understates rather than overstates, but it is not a fixed number and USJ's
   own site publishes it only through a date calendar. The Express Pass is genuinely a separate
   ticket (from roughly 11,800 yen), which is what the slide actually claims.
2. **Golden Gai seat charges (slide 8, "500 to 1,500 yen").** Each bar is its own business and there
   is no association price list, so this range comes from current visitor guides rather than one
   primary operator. It is well attested but treat it as a range, not a quoted fee.
3. **Ebisu Yokocho "about 2,000 yen for the night" (slide 8).** A typical spend figure from guides,
   not a published price. The verifiable part, roughly 20 stalls around shared tables in a converted
   Showa market, is solid.
4. **Kiyomizu-dera 500 yen (slide 3).** The temple's own English site has no fee page, so this is
   confirmed across current guides rather than from the temple directly. It has been 500 yen for
   years, but it is the one number in the deck without a primary citation.
5. **The Fuji Lawson barrier is a moving target (slide 9).** The original 2.5 m screen went up in
   May 2024, came down that August, and a lower 1.4 m barrier is what is there now. The slide says
   "a barrier up to keep you off the road", which holds for the current arrangement, but this one
   could date faster than anything else in the deck.
6. **Two takes are deliberately scoped, not blanket.** Slide 3 targets the Sannenzaka approach and
   the crowds, not the temple's worth, and slide 5 targets the Nakamise souvenir lane, not Sensoji
   itself. Both are written that way on purpose to pre-empt the obvious "well actually", and the
   caption spells the distinction out.
