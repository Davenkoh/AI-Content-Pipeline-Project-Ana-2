## Unverified / caveats for the human

1. **Superrich downtown vs airport.** The slide says use Superrich rather than the arrivals-hall bank
   counters, which is solidly true. Sources also say the downtown branches are very slightly better
   than the airport branch (roughly 0.30 to 0.50 THB per USD), which the copy does not get into. No
   overclaim, just noting the nuance.
2. **Cover-up price at the Grand Palace** (100 to 200 baht) comes from visitor guides rather than an
   official palace page, and there is some variation between rent and buy. The 500 baht entry is
   solid.
3. **Maya Bay entry fee** is reported at 400 baht for 2026 but is not quoted on the slide, so nothing
   rides on it.
4. **The tuk tuk scam's current footprint.** One source says tourist police stationed opposite the
   palace have pushed it off that exact corner. The copy is written as "don't believe the guy who
   says the palace is closed", which holds wherever he is standing.
5. **Elephant Nature Park operating status** was not confirmed against its own booking page this run.
   Worth a look before this goes out, since it is the only named venue in the deck.
