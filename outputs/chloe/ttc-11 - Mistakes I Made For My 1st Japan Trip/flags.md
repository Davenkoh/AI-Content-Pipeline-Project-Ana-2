## Two time-sensitivity / nuance notes (surface to the human)
1. **Tax-free mechanism changes 2026-11-01.** Under the CURRENT system (valid now, July 2026) tax is deducted
   at point of sale / refunded at a store desk. From **Nov 1, 2026** Japan switches to pay-tax-then-refund-on-
   departure (source: japan-guide.com/news/tax-free-shopping). The slide + caption were deliberately written
   **timing-neutral** ("skip the 10% tax", "you can skip the ten percent consumption tax") so they stay true
   under both systems — the ¥5,000+ threshold, passport requirement, and ~10% saving are unchanged. No number
   is wrong today; just flagging the shelf-life if this reposts after November.
2. **Escalator = practical custom, not the current official ask.** Tokyo-left / Osaka-right is the lived,
   widely-true custom and the real tourist mistake (blocking the walking side). Note that stations are now
   officially campaigning for "stand still on BOTH sides, hold the handrail" (getaroundjapan.jp, Time Out
   Tokyo). The slide teaches the practical reality travelers will actually encounter; treated as OPINION/
   etiquette anchored to the verified regional fact. No change recommended.
