## Verified (with confidence)
- **Sa Pa → Ha Giang (slide 2):** Sa Pa is now heavily developed for tourism (concrete hotels, tour agencies, vendor persistence, large daily crowds, "packaged" feel). The Ha Giang Loop is remote and much less touristy, a multi-day motorbike route; the Ma Pi Leng pass view over the Nho Que river gorge is repeatedly ranked "among the best views in Vietnam." Slide claims = HIGH (qualitative but well-documented).
- **Ben Thanh → Ho Thi Ky (slide 3):** Ben Thanh is the flagship tourist market (marked-up prices, aggressive hard-sell haggling) — judgment, widely documented. Ho Thi Ky = a ~300m local food alley in District 10 with ~125 stalls (Cambodian-Vietnamese food); grilled skewers from ~10,000 VND and dishes ~15,000-50,000 VND (~$0.60-2). Slide "skewers from about a dollar" = HIGH (cheapest skewers ~$0.40-0.60; "from about a dollar" is a safe round-up).
- **Nha Trang → Quy Nhon (slide 5):** Nha Trang = a high-energy resort city, a wall of beachfront high-rises, water parks + a cable car, heavy tourism infrastructure. Quy Nhon = the laid-back alternative, quiet beautiful beaches (Ky Co, Eo Gio), cheap fresh seafood, minimal over-tourism. Slide claims = HIGH.
- **Phu Quoc → Con Dao (slide 6):** Phu Quoc now hosts Grand World (Vietnam's largest theme park) and the Hon Thom cable car (recognized as the world's longest non-stop three-rope cable car, ~8 km over the sea); tourism target ~15M by 2030; a common visitor complaint is that the mega-development "feels so fake." Con Dao = a remote, pristine, unspoiled archipelago and national park. Slide "giant theme park and cable car" + "pristine, quiet islands and a national park" = HIGH.
- **Mekong day tour → Can Tho (slide 7):** Cai Rang floating market (near Can Tho) is most lively ~5:00-7:30 AM and winds down / slows after ~9:00 AM ("at 9am you are mostly watching other tourists watch it"). A day tour from Saigon (~3-4 h each way) arrives well after the real wholesale trading, and those tours are largely a My Tho coconut-candy + handicraft-workshop circuit. Slide "arrive too late... staged candy-shop stop" + "hit Cai Rang at sunrise, it's over by 9am" = HIGH.
