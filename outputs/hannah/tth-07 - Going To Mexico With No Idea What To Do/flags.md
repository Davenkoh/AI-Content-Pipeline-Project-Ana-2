## Verified (with confidence)
- **Chichen Itza ~697 pesos, two tickets at the gate (slide 8):** the 2026 foreign-adult total is about 697 MXN, split into a federal INAH ticket (~105 MXN) and a Yucatan state CULTUR ticket (~592 MXN), normally paid at two separate booths. Foreign children 3 to 12 pay ~105 MXN; Mexican nationals ~298 MXN with INE; Yucatan residents ~105 MXN with ID. The slide's "about 697 pesos all in, 2 tickets at the gate" is accurate including the two-booth detail. Confidence HIGH.
- **Holbox whale sharks, June to September, peaking July and August (slide 12):** tours run June through September with the official season window roughly 15 May to 17 September; July and August are peak, and boats are capped at 8 to 10 passengers departing early. The slide claims only the months. Confidence HIGH.
- **Teotihuacan has no shade (slide 3):** the site is an open, exposed archaeological zone with essentially no shade, which is why opening time is the standard advice. Qualitative, well documented. Confidence HIGH.
- **Frida Kahlo Museum sells out / book online ahead (slide 3):** timed online tickets and frequent sell-outs are consistently documented. The slide makes no price claim. Confidence HIGH.
- **Ek Balam is still climbable (slide 8):** Ek Balam's Acropolis remains one of the few Yucatan pyramids visitors may still ascend, unlike Chichen Itza. Confidence HIGH.
